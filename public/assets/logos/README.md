# Brand logo assets

- `x.svg`, `facebook.svg`, and `telegram.svg` are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), released under CC0-1.0. Their paths use the corresponding brand colors.
- `linkedin.svg` is the LinkedIn glyph from [Font Awesome Free](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome), released as an icon under CC BY 4.0.
- `note.svg` is the small-size icon from the [official note visual identity assets](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360000235582).
- `instagram.svg` is the pure SVG gradient glyph archived by [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Instagram_Glyph_Gradient_RGB_logo.svg), sourced there from Meta's official Instagram brand resources.

Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Inclusion here does not imply endorsement.
